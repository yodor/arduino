LDR daughter board firmware -- 2026-10-12b (driver v1a, sweep dark edge: deeper target, more passes, re-read)  (driver v1a)

Sweep dark edge: the edge now aims at ~1 Mohm (a 500k reference, was 150k), may take up to
7 halving passes (was 4), and re-reads a timed-out "unreadable" point next to the edge with
a settled dwell. On a cool board the knee sits a few counts higher, and the old edge stopped
at ~295k by chance, capping RMAX. Adds roughly 10 s to a full characterization.

1. DELETE your project's src/ folder, copy this package's src/ next to platformio.ini.
2. pio run -t clean ; pio run ; flash .pio/build/picow/firmware.uf2 (BOOTSEL + copy).
3. Boot: no format change, the stored calibration is kept. To re-measure the reach:
     DEBUG, FORGET, CAL       (ideally on a warm board, ~40 min after power-up)
