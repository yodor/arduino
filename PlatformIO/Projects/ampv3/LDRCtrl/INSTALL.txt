LDR attenuator firmware -- 2026-11-01c  (driver v1b, 2nd-edition LDR boards)

The cells wander about +-2 counts with their recent light history (the same on both channels;
VERIFY #14-#16 showed it). The firmware no longer reads that as an error:
* VERIFY verdict: RESYNC only beyond 3 counts (settled quiet point), clear of the wander.
* CHANGES judges the compensation from a temperature step only when the shift it should cause
  exceeds the wander (>= 4 counts); smaller changes are reported as within the wander.
* Fix: the settled step probes read the series with the shunt held bright when the shunt is too
  dark to pass a measurable current (R at steps 25/28 was "unreadable"), as the step verify does.
* README, Limitations: the wander as a known property (absolute level +-1.5-2 dB at the quiet and
  middle steps, L/R balance unaffected).
