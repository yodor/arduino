LDR daughter board firmware -- 2026-10-24a (master-controlled power-up: muted until told; boot only checks)  (driver v1b, 2nd-edition LDR boards)

Protocol revision 10 -- the master owns the power state:
* Power-up: amp mute ON and LDR mute ON, until the master changes them. No timed boot hold,
  no automatic unmute (MUTE_BOOT_HOLD_MS is gone).
* Power-up only CHECKS (cell check ~10 s, STATUS=BUSY, PROGRESS PHASE=CHECK). Nothing is
  calibrated automatically. STATUS then says what is needed:
    CAL=OK       ready
    CAL=NONE     nothing stored               -> CAL runs a full characterization
    CAL=CHANGED  other cells than calibrated  -> CAL runs a full characterization
    CAL=STALE    Rtotal policy / model changed -> CAL re-solves (quick)
* Until CAL=OK: the LDR mute stays on; VOL ... and MUTE OFF reply
  "ERR calibration needed (CAL=...)". AMP_MUTE, MUTE ON, CAL, FORGET and the queries work.
* A calibration never unmutes anything; FORGET CAL engages the LDR mute first.
See daughter-board-uart-protocol-v2.md, "Power-up and standby", for the recommended master
sequence (standby: AMP_MUTE ON + SSR off, leave the LDR at its step).

The bench console (DEBUG) is unrestricted, as a bench tool: its VOL works even when CAL is
not OK.

firmware.uf2 is a real build: hold BOOTSEL, plug in, copy it to RPI-RP2. Or build from src/.
No recalibration: same file format; calibration, learning log and references are kept.
