LDR daughter board firmware -- 2026-10-28d  (driver v1b, 2nd-edition LDR boards)

Balance (protocol 18): BAL <-16..16> in 0.25 dB units (+-4 dB); negative lowers the right channel,
positive the left; BAL alone replies OK BAL=<n>. At 0 both channels play exactly their calibrated
steps. The lowered channel plays between its steps, solved for the exact level under the table's
own rules (within ~0.01 dB of the requested level on your table). Applies to volume changes, the
ramp, MUTE OFF and after calibrations. Not stored: the master sends it after power-up.
The volume ramp stays: 2 ms per step (a full-range jump ~60 ms), a free guard against pops.
Everything from 2026-10-28c (temperature-spread learning log) included.

firmware.uf2 is a real build: hold BOOTSEL, plug in, copy it to RPI-RP2. Or build from src/.
No recalibration needed. NEW FILES: Balance.cpp / Balance.hpp -- copy the whole src/ folder.
