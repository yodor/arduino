LDR attenuator firmware -- 2026-10-30k  (driver v1b, 2nd-edition LDR boards)

* Resync hint, state-dependent warm-up hold:
  - LEARNING (no trusted compensation): no warm-up hold. RESYNC=1 WHY=NOCOMP as soon as the block
    has moved 1 C since the last calibration -- warm-up included, that is the data the learning
    needs. Only a block moving faster than 1 C per 10 min is held (WARMING).
  - COMPENSATION ACTIVE: held 10 min after the amp is switched on and while the block moves more
    than 0.5 C per 10 min (was 15 min / 0.25 C, which held a slowly warming enclosure for hours).
* A characterization now ends with the resync's middle-depth refinement (~30 s more): on a cold
  start the block warms during it, and its middle steps were driving the cells brighter than
  needed at the end (too loud) until the first resync.
Protocol unchanged (20). Tests: python3 test/run_tests.py (22).
